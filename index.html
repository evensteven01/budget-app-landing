Most budgeting apps show you a bank balance, or make you think in calendar months. If you're paid every two weeks (or weekly, or on the 15th/30th), that's the wrong shape for your actual money — a three-paycheck month throws most budgeting apps off completely. This one re-draws the window around your real payday every time.

Is this for you?

A good fit
  • Paid weekly, biweekly, or semimonthly
  • Quit YNAB because categorizing every transaction got old
  • Don't want to link your bank account to anything
  • Want to actually understand your money, not just see a pie chart
Probably not
  • Want full auto-sync, zero manual entry
  • Just want a passive balance widget

What testing actually involves

  • A short setup session — add your pay schedule, a couple of accounts, and your recurring bills. Real numbers or made-up ones, entirely your call.
  • Normal use for 1–2 pay periods — the whole point only shows up once you check it before a real purchase and watch the number roll over at your next payday.
  • Two short check-ins — one after setup, one after a week or two. A few questions, not a survey marathon.

On privacy: the app runs fully on your device — no servers, no bank login, nothing you enter is ever sent anywhere. If you'd still rather not type in real account balances, made-up numbers test the app exactly as well.
